The Armoury

Three things worth having, and very few of them in the house.

Fewer than a dozen tokens exist across the entire day, spread over thirteen people. They are the scarcest thing here, and they are won in challenges — or handed to you by the dead.

The Shield

Protects you from murder for one night. Tell the host privately before the night window closes.

Some shields are hollow. Identical to look at, identical to hold, and they do nothing whatsoever. You find out which kind you had on the morning it mattered.

The Dagger

Your vote counts twice at the next Circle. Declared openly when you read your card — so everyone watches you spend it, and everyone forms a view about why.

The Mask

The strongest thing in the game and the rarest. Only one exists in the entire game, and it does not appear until the fourth session.

Name one player privately to the host. You are told, truthfully, whether they are Faithful or a Traitor.

Masks never lie. Nobody has to believe you.

A token you claim is not a token you hold. Everyone in this house can say the word "mask".
