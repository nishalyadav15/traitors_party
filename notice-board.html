The game

Who's who
Some of the thirteen are Traitors. The rest are Faithful. You are never told how many, and the number can change.
How to win
Faithful win if every Traitor is gone. Traitors win if even one survives.
Each session
A challenge, then the Circle of Shaq where the group banishes someone by vote, then a night where the Traitors murder someone.
Being banished
You reveal what you were, on the spot, and then you never speak about it again.
Session One
No banishment, and the Traitors don't know each other yet. They find out at the first night.
◆

The one rule people break

Phones out during recess and during the night window. Phones face-down during challenges and the whole Circle of Shaq.

At night, everyone is on their phone at their own spot. That's what lets the Traitors talk without being spotted, so it only works if everyone does it. Touch your phone during a challenge or the Circle and you lose your vote at the next Circle.

The three tokens

Shield
Survive one night's murder. Some shields are hollow and do nothing — you won't know until the morning it mattered.
Dagger
Your vote counts twice, declared openly at the Circle.
Mask
Learn one player's true allegiance. Only one exists in the whole game. It never lies, and nobody has to believe you.

Fewer than a dozen tokens exist in the whole house. Anyone can claim to hold one.

◆

If you die

You keep playing
You still win or lose with your original team, so keep caring.
You compete
The dead play every challenge in their own bracket.
You give
Whatever you win goes to a living player of your choosing. The room is told who you picked, never what you gave.
You say nothing
Nothing you learned while alive leaves your mouth. Your gifts are your only voice.
You might return
Twice in the day the dead vote to resurrect a Faithful. They come back with a freshly drawn role, which may not be the one they had, and nobody knows what it is.
◆

How it ends

When the room is small, everyone left votes secretly to end the game or banish once more. It takes everyone agreeing to stop. Three rounds of that at most.

If one Traitor is still breathing when the fire goes out, the Traitors take everything.

◆

Three things worth knowing

  1. Don't try to count. You don't know how many Traitors there are or whether that number has moved. Play the people instead.
  2. You will banish innocent people. Everyone does. A nervous Faithful looks exactly like a Traitor.
  3. The dead are not neutral. Their gifts are arguments, and one of them wants you to lose.
