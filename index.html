The premise

Among the thirteen of you, some are Traitors. The rest are Faithful.

Each night, the Traitors murder a Faithful. Each day, the group banishes one player by vote — hoping they've caught a Traitor.

  • The Faithful win if every Traitor is gone by the end.
  • The Traitors win if a single one of them is still standing.

You will never be told how many Traitors there are. Not at the start, not in the middle, not when you ask the host directly. And the number will not necessarily be the same at the end as it was at the beginning.

Do not try to solve this game with arithmetic. It doesn't work here.

Nine things that will help you

  1. You cannot count your way out. You don't know how many there are, and you don't know whether that's changed. Play the people.
  2. The loudest accuser in the first session is usually Faithful. Usually.
  3. Watch who benefits from each murder, not who reacts hardest to it.
  4. A nervous Faithful is indistinguishable from a Traitor. You will banish innocent people. Everyone does. It isn't a moral failure.
  5. Claimed tokens are not proven tokens. Anyone can say they hold a Mask. Anyone can say they were shielded.
  6. The dead are not neutral and they are not silent. Their gifts are arguments. Somebody out there is lying to you with a shield.
  7. Traitors have to hunt Traitors too. Being helpful proves nothing. Neither does suspecting the right person.
  8. Notice who the Traitors don't kill. Surviving night after night is a fact about you that other people can see.
  9. Nobody here is being serious about you personally. Remember that around five o'clock, when it stops feeling like a game.

House rules

  • Phones down during challenges and the Circle. Full stop.
  • Never show anyone your role message. Never photograph it.
  • The banished say nothing after their reveal, for the rest of the day.
  • The dead speak only through their gifts.
  • The host's ruling is instant and final. Argue about it over dinner.
