The Round Table

Where the day is divided, and where you will be asked to name someone.

How Saturday runs

1:00

Arrival. Food, drinks, catching up.

Nothing is happening yet. Enjoy it — it's the last honest fifteen minutes of the day.

1:15

The Selection.

Every phone in the house buzzes at the same moment. Everyone receives a message. Read it alone, tell nobody, come back.

1:30

Session One begins.

The day then runs in sessions of about fifty minutes, with a break in the middle, until the endgame around seven.

◆

The session

Session One is different

There is a challenge. There is recess. And then there is the first night.

There is no Circle of Shaq on the first day. Nobody is banished. The first blood is a murder, and there is nothing any of you can do to prevent it.

There's one more thing about Session One, and you'll only appreciate it afterwards: the Traitors do not know each other yet. They find out at the first night and not one second before. Whatever a Traitor does in the first session, they do alone, in a room where anyone might be their partner.

Every session after that

I

The Morning.

The host announces who was murdered. That player is Faithful — the Traitors never kill their own. They leave the circle without speaking.

II

Recess. Five minutes.

Phones out. Talk to anyone, in person or by text. Alliances get built here and stories get straightened.

III

The Challenge. Eighteen minutes.

A group task. Winners earn tokens — the only real power in this game. Phones away.

IV

Recess. Five minutes.

Phones out again. Regroup, compare notes, panic quietly.

V

The Circle of Shaq. Twelve minutes.

Everyone in the circle. Phones face-down and they stay there.

Discussion, accusation, defence. Then everyone writes one name on a card. Going round the circle, each player holds up their card, reads the name aloud, and gives a reason.

The player with the most votes is banished, and must immediately reveal whether they were Faithful or a Traitor. They say nothing else, about anything, ever.

Ties are re-voted between the tied players only. A second tie goes to the host's coin.

VI

The Night. Eight minutes.

Everyone goes to their designated spot. Phones out. Silence in the room.

The Traitors have eight minutes to agree on a name and send it to the host. Then the house sleeps.

The first night runs twelve minutes. The Traitors have more to talk about than you'd think.

◆

Phone rules

Phones out

During recess, and during the night window.

Phones face-down

During challenges, and throughout the Circle of Shaq.

The night window is the one that matters. Every person in this house is on their phone for those eight minutes, alone at their own spot, saying nothing out loud. Furious typing means nothing. A blank face means nothing. Someone laughing at their screen means nothing.

That is precisely the point.

Touching your phone during a challenge or the Circle costs you your vote at the next Circle. Not negotiable, and the host will not be talked round.

◆

The endgame

When the room gets small, the fire is lit.

Each surviving player votes secretly on paper: end or banish.

  • Everyone votes end — the game stops and all roles are revealed.
  • Even one banish — an open vote, a banishment, a reveal, and the choice comes round again.

Three cycles maximum, then the fire goes out regardless.

If one Traitor is still breathing when it does, the Traitors take everything.
