The Summons

One thing to send on Friday. One place to be on Saturday.

The Friday brief

Every one of the sixteen sends the host the same thing, the day before. Everyone is asked. Everyone answers.

In thirty to fifty words: would you rather be a Traitor or Faithful, and why? Be honest — it isn't a vote, and the host decides.

The button opens WhatsApp with the host, with a starting line already in it — finish the sentence and send. Don't discuss your answer with anyone else; what you claim to have written is your business on Saturday.
